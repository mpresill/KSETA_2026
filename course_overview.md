# Course Overview

## KSETA PhD Course 2026: EFT and Experimental Measurements

### Course Objectives

By the end of this course, students will:

1. Understand the fundamental principles of Effective Field Theory (EFT)
2. Learn how to apply EFT to practical problems in particle physics
3. Gain hands-on experience with experimental measurement techniques
4. Develop skills in analyzing experimental data

### Course Structure

The course is divided into several modules:

#### Module 1: Introduction to Effective Field Theory
- Basics of EFT
- Power counting and dimensional analysis
- Matching and running

#### Module 2: Experimental Measurements
- Detector principles
- Data collection methods
- Systematic uncertainties
- Statistical analysis

### Learning Format

The course uses an interactive approach combining:
- **Lectures**: Theoretical foundations and concepts
- **Jupyter Notebooks**: Hands-on coding exercises
- **Problem Sets**: Practice problems and assignments
- **Discussions**: Collaborative learning sessions

### Assessment

- Participation in exercises
- Completion of notebook assignments
- Final project
